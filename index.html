Projects

AstraSync

Since AI 2026, Traficom challenge. Built with Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, DuckDB, React and TypeScript. github.com/SCP619/AstraSync

Radio spectrum screening that reads the ITU's fortnightly frequency notices and finds the few that could interfere with radio use in Finland, turning them into ranked cases with full provenance for expert review. I ported the ITU-R P.1546-6 propagation model and validated it against 54 ITU reference cases, and screened 1,867 terrestrial and 196 space notices down to 15 and 55 items for review. A local language model drafts answers from case evidence, with citations and numbers checked server-side, and the platform ships as web, Windows and Android builds with more than 400 automated tests.

Antibiotic Efficacy Indicator (AEI)

JunctionX Turku 2026, Tyks Laboratories challenge. Built with Python, DuckDB, FastAPI and React.

Ward-level antimicrobial resistance reporting that publishes every rate with its denominator, Wilson confidence interval and suppression reason, rather than hiding small-sample cells. I validated it against an independently written pipeline of 99 tests, including 48 golden-value and 29 parity checks, which caught two real defects. Alert text is derived from the statistics, not generated by a language model.

PuckLights

Ice hockey highlight generation. Lead contributor in a team of five. Built with YOLO and Flutter.

Real-time computer vision pipeline that detects goals and saves in live or recorded footage and assembles them into highlight reels. I owned the systems work: running inference fast enough for live play on a single RTX GPU, then parallelising it across concurrent streams and users.
